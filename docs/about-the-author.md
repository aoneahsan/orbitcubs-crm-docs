OrbitCubs CRM is built and maintained by **Ahsan Mahmood** — a full-stack
engineer who builds free-tier, client-side web and mobile products and publishes
open-source packages for the JavaScript and Capacitor ecosystems.

## About OrbitCubs CRM

OrbitCubs CRM is part of a broader portfolio of products designed around a

teams and solo operators can use it without a subscription. If OrbitCubs CRM is
