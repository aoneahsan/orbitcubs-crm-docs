# Products, Quotes & Orders

The selling side of the CRM connects a catalog to the deals you close.

## Products

The **product catalog** holds the things you sell — names, descriptions, and
pricing — so quotes and orders draw from a single, consistent source rather than
free-typed line items.

## Quotes

A **quote** assembles catalog products into a priced proposal tied to an
opportunity, account, and contact. Quotes let you put a concrete number in front
of a prospect and track which version they accepted.

## Orders

When a quote is accepted, it becomes an **order** — the record of what was
actually sold. Orders close the loop from opportunity → quote → order, so revenue
is captured against the deal that produced it.
