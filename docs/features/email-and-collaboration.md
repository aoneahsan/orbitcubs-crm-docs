# Email & Collaboration

Reach customers and keep your team in sync.

## Email

OrbitCubs CRM supports **email templates** with merge fields and **automation
sequences** that send a series of messages over time. A composer ties email to
your records so outreach stays in context.

:::info Google-only integration
Email integration is **Google-only** today (the documented zero-setup path).
Outlook integration is intentionally not implemented yet because it requires a
separate Microsoft OAuth app registration. This is an honest current limitation,
not a hidden gap.
:::

## Collaboration feed

An internal **feed** lets your team post updates, share context, and run quick
**polls**. It's the lightweight, in-app place to communicate without leaving the
CRM.

## Messaging

**Direct messages** and group threads let members talk one-to-one or in small
groups. (Mentions currently display a short user identifier; richer display-name
resolution is a planned enhancement — see the [Changelog](/changelog).)
