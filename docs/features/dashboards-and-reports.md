# Dashboards & Reports

Turn your records into a picture of the business.

## Dashboards

The **dashboard builder** lets you assemble views from your data — pipeline
health, case load, activity volume — and save them for repeat use. You can list,
build, and view dashboards, so different roles can keep the metrics that matter
to them.

## Reports

**Reports** run structured queries over your records and present the results in
tables (including grouped rows). Reports are the detailed, row-level counterpart
to the at-a-glance dashboards.

## Charts

Visualizations are rendered with **D3** for full control over how data is drawn,
which keeps charts consistent and performant across the app rather than depending
on a heavyweight charting library.
