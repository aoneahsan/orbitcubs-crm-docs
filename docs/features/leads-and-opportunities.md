# Leads & Opportunities

This is where prospects become revenue.

## Leads

A **lead** is an unqualified prospect — someone who has shown interest but isn't
yet a committed deal. Leads capture source, contact details, and status so you
can triage and qualify them. Qualified leads convert into a contact/account plus
an opportunity.

## Opportunities

An **opportunity** is a deal in progress. Opportunities track amount, stage,
expected close date, and the account/contacts involved, and they move through a
**pipeline** so you can see what's likely to close and when.

## Predictive lead scoring

OrbitCubs CRM ships **predictive lead scoring** that ranks leads by their
likelihood to convert, computed client-side from your own data so you can focus
on the prospects most worth your time.

## Forecasting & quotas

Pipeline rolls up into **forecasting** with quota tracking, so you can compare
expected revenue against targets. (Forecasting and territory/quota tooling are
part of the advanced module set — see the [Changelog](/changelog) for what's
shipped.)
