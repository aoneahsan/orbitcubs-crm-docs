# Contacts & Accounts

Contacts and accounts are the backbone of the CRM.

## Contacts

A **contact** is a person you do business with. Each contact carries the usual
identity and channel details, can be linked to an account, and accumulates a
history of related activities, cases, and deals.

## Accounts

An **account** represents a company or organization. Contacts roll up to
accounts so you can see everyone you know at a given company in one place, along
with that company's opportunities, cases, orders, and quotes.

## Customer 360

The **Customer 360** view brings together everything tied to a customer —
profile details, open and won opportunities, support cases, recent activities,
and segment membership — so you don't have to jump between modules to understand
a relationship.

## Segments

**Segments** let you group contacts or accounts by shared criteria (for example,
industry, stage, or region) so you can target outreach, reporting, and campaigns
consistently.
