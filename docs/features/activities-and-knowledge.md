# Activities & Knowledge

The everyday work and reference material that surrounds your records.

## Activities

**Activities** are the things you do with customers — tasks, calls, meetings, and
notes. They attach to contacts, accounts, opportunities, and cases so every
record shows its own timeline of what happened and what's next.

## Attachments

**Attachments** let you store files against records. Uploads go through
**FilesHub** rather than paid object storage, keeping the project within its
free-tier, zero-cost constraints while still giving each record its documents.

## Knowledge base

The **knowledge base** holds articles organized into categories, with search so
your team can find answers fast. Articles support viewing, editing, and category
management — useful both for internal playbooks and for support agents resolving
[cases](/features/cases-and-support).
