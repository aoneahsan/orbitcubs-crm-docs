# AI & Automation

OrbitCubs CRM includes intelligence and automation that run within the app's
zero-cost, client-side constraints.

## AI assistant

An in-app **assistant** helps you work with your CRM data conversationally. It is
built to operate on free-tier infrastructure rather than a paid cloud AI tier.

## Predictive scoring & churn

Two predictive features ship today:

- **Predictive lead scoring** ranks leads by likelihood to convert.
- **Churn prediction** flags accounts at risk of leaving.

Both are computed from your own data using client-side libraries, so there is no
per-request AI bill.

## Workflows

The **workflow builder** lets you define automations visually — trigger →
conditions → actions — and manage them from a workflow list and viewer. Use it to
remove repetitive manual steps from your process.

## Approvals

The **approval builder** models multi-step approval chains (for example, on
quotes or discounts), so requests route to the right people before they proceed.

:::note Advanced module roadmap
Beyond the shipped set above, OrbitCubs CRM has backend service scaffolding for a
broader catalog of advanced modules (for example data enrichment, SMS, and video
calling). Several of those depend on third-party providers and are not built into
the UI yet. The [Changelog](/changelog) tracks what is actually shipped.
:::
